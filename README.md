# majorMap

majorMap is a tool that helps college students understand the economic outcomes and trade-offs of different majors.

## Research Question

How does a student's choice of major effect their earnings, employment stability, debt, and career flexibility?

## Economic Dimensions

- Earnings
- Earnings growth
- Unemployed
- Underemployment
- Debt weight

## Version 1 scope

- Compare economic outcomes across majors
- Early-career earnings
- Mid-career earnings
- Earnings Growth
- Unemployment rates
- Underemployment rates
- Student debt
- Risks and trade-offs
- Preference based matchings

## Not in Version 1

- Graduate school stats
- University-specific recs
- Admissions data
- AI-generated recommendations
